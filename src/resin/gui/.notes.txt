Final recommendations
    Keep TYPE_CHECKING and the few method-local imports needed to break real cycles.
    Do not permit Container.find() to construct connections or sessions.
    Delegate actual lookup to ComGateway.find_component().
    Preserve SapComponentNotFoundError rather than converting it into SapComError.
    Make component_cls and _raise keyword-only.
    Use Literal overloads for precise return typing.
    Replace duplicated static validators with returning functions in utils.validation.
    Move COM apartment and message-pump behavior to com.runtime.
    Avoid silent exception suppression, especially around COM initialization and pumping.
    Add static type-checking tests for overload behavior, in addition to runtime tests.
